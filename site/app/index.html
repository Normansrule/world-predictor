Can a machine do Hari Seldon's psychohistory?

Google's open-source TimesFM forecasts world news, markets (stocks, gold, silver) and new technologies. Every forecast is dated before the future happens and scored once it arrives.

About World Predictor

Two ways to use it

On the web (this page): nothing to install. GitHub Pages serves the app with the newest weekly forecasts.

On your computer: the full version runs TimesFM yourself and opens this same app on your machine.

psychohistory app builds and opens it at http://localhost:8000.

How it is scored

Skill: 1 − error(model) ÷ error(naive). 80% range: should catch reality 8 times in 10. Odds score (Brier): 0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin flip. Backtests never let a model see the future it is tested on.

Nothing here is financial advice.
